## 1 · « Six gestes » — PICKED, with music + haptic sound and no voice (recommended: a teacher can repeat the task after one viewing, and every screen is the real app)

**Device / form:** the result first, then an object-to-object chain.

The published communiqué drops into the list with a haptic thump and « Opération réussie », then the film rewinds and shows how, one step at a time. The iPad sits on the brand grid; the camera punches in on every tap, each tap lands with a touch ring, a squash-and-settle of the control and a 3 px micro-shake, and the tapped control lifts off the screen as an amber chip that wipes into the next step.

- **First 2 s:** frame 0 = the list card with the green toast already on screen; 0.4 s the card thumps (1.06 → 1, shake) on a click; 1.2 s « Un DS annoncé à 2 classes » slams in; 1.8 s fast rewind with a whoosh.
- **Beats:** hook (result) → rewind to Accueil → 1 Menu › Communiqué évaluatif → 2 Classes T.C.S.F1 + T.C.S.F2 → 3 Type DS → 4 Matière SVT → 5 Date 04/11/2026 → 6 Titre & message → Envoyer → payoff (the card lands, the six chips stack into it) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** `#0D1316` + 60 px hairline grid; the real iPad screen in a thin dark bezel (the app keeps its own navy and blue); amber only on the current tap (ring, chip, step number); Space Grotesk step titles in the top band, Inter labels.
- **Sound:** brand synth bed (90 BPM, D minor); soft click + low thump on every tap, pop on each chip, whoosh on each step change (2–3 takes rotated), one riser into the impact on « Envoyer ».
- **Length:** about 40 s.

## 2 · « Mercredi 4 novembre »

**Device / form:** a moment in a teacher's day, one continuous take; one object (the message) travels through it.

It opens on the teacher's real morning, 08:59, mer. 14 oct., the day's timetable from the home page blown up as a giant timeline, and a note types itself: « DS le 4 novembre · prévenir les deux classes ». The camera never cuts: it slides along the timeline into the iPad, the two class rows become the selected classes, the calendar rolls from octobre to novembre, and the real message travels as a card into the form, then lands on 04 nov. for both classes.

- **First 2 s:** frame 0 = giant « 08:59 » + « mer. 14 oct. » over the SVT timetable; 1.0 s the T.C.S.F1 and T.C.S.F2 rows turn amber; 1.5 s the note starts typing.
- **Beats:** morning timetable → the need (the note) → into the iPad → classes taken from the timetable → DS / SVT → calendar rolls to 4 nov. → the message card flies in → Envoyer → the card pinned on 04 nov. for both classes → claim « Un message. Deux classes. Une date. » → end card.
- **Look:** same ground and grid; the timetable and calendar redrawn large in brand type from the app's real data; the iPad screens as the proof; amber only on the travelling message card.
- **Sound:** the brand bed, sparse at first (pad + arpeggio), bass enters when the iPad appears; key ticks on the typed note, a paper-slide whoosh for the card, one impact when it pins.
- **Length:** about 45 s.

## 3 · « Tap, tap, envoyé » (the bold one)

**Device / form:** type carries the argument, cut on the beat.

A fast social reel: each step is one giant word slammed full-frame on a beat (CLASSES · TYPE · MATIÈRE · DATE · MESSAGE), immediately broken open by a 1 s punch-in of the real screen doing exactly that, with a haptic hit on each tap (shake + a one-frame flash). Colour fields alternate between the dark grid and solid amber; ENVOYÉ lands on the drop with the success toast.

- **First 2 s:** frame 0 = « UN DS ? » huge in amber on the dark grid; beat 1 (0.67 s) hard cut to a full amber field « 04 NOV. »; beat 2 hard cut to the iPad punch-in.
- **Beats:** hook → CLASSES → TYPE → MATIÈRE → DATE → MESSAGE → ENVOYÉ (drop) → the card in the list → end card.
- **Look:** full-bleed fields alternating `#0D1316` + grid and solid `#F4A62A` (text on amber `#0D1316`); Space Grotesk 700 at 220–320 px; UI crops full-bleed with 14 px corners.
- **Sound:** the brand bed in its full groove from frame 0 (kick on every beat); a thump on each slam, whoosh only on field swaps (rotated takes), riser + impact on ENVOYÉ.
- **Length:** 25–30 s.
