# Sound (not in this repository)

The soundtrack mixes the audio of an Instagram clip by Carlo Teope ("And for my next trick…", 0–22.8 s) with
Coldplay, *Viva La Vida* (2008). Both are third-party, copyrighted recordings: they and every file mixed from them
(`assets/src/audio/`, `assets/audio/`, rendered MP4s) are git-ignored and stay on the machine that renders the film.
